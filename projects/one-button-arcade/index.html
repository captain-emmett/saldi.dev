The idea

Small hardware.
A little competition.

An Adafruit QT Py ESP32-S3, a 128 × 64 OLED display, and a mechanical key switch. Four quick presses start a ten-second speed round; the five fastest scores are saved even when the power is off.

The same button handles initials entry and high-score browsing through press timing and debouncing. Outside a round, the display tracks the lifetime button-press count.

I designed and 3D-printed a case around the display, switch, microcontroller, and wiring, then tested the assembled device. Startup diagnostics help identify display and wiring issues.
