The idea

Find your bearings.
Make your own map.

The concept pairs a Raspberry Pi 5 and a small touch display with GPS and orientation sensors. The goal is to explore the sky around you, recognize familiar constellations, and connect stars into patterns of your own.

This project is in its early concept and architecture phase. A software star-map prototype and hardware integration are the next steps.
