The idea

Explore the sky.
Save what you see.

I’m developing a dedicated device for exploring the night sky and making constellations of your own. The intended experience is to find stars, connect them into a pattern, give it a name and story, and save it locally.

The Rust and Macroquad prototype displays a real bright-star catalog with touch and mouse controls. GPS and orientation sensors are planned to align the view with the device’s location and direction; the software currently uses a development location.

Hardware work includes component-fit and enclosure prototypes. Sensor integration, portable power, cooling, and outdoor testing remain part of the work ahead.
