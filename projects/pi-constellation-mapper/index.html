The idea

Find your bearings.
Make your own map.

I'm developing a portable device for exploring the visible night sky and drawing, naming, and saving custom constellations through a touch interface.

The prototype uses a Raspberry Pi 5, a 5-inch Touch Display 2, a GPS module, a BNO085 orientation sensor, and a custom interface in Rust.

Work so far includes touch and kiosk behavior, sensor planning, power and cooling needs, and 3D-printed enclosure prototypes. The project is still in progress, with component fit and usability guiding the hardware design.
