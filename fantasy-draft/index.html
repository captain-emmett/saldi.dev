How the recommendation score works

It starts with the selected scoring rank, then adjusts for how far a player has fallen relative to the current pick, open starter slots, positional tier scarcity, your starred targets, bye-week concentration and your selected draft strategy. Kickers and defenses are intentionally suppressed until the late rounds unless you filter directly to those positions. This is a decision aid—not a projection model.
